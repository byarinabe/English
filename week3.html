📚 Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple

Use it for:
  • habits and routines
  • regular activities
  • facts and general preferences
Form:

I/you/we/they + V1
he/she/it + V-s

I usually listen to music in the evening.
My best friend plays volleyball.
Markers:

always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, once a week, at weekends

Present Continuous

Use it for:
  • actions happening now
  • temporary situations around now
Form:

am/is/are + verb-ing

I’m reading a new book at the moment.
My friend is learning Spanish this month.
Markers:

now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, these days

Compare

I play tennis every Saturday. — regular habit (Present Simple).
I’m playing tennis now. — happening now (Present Continuous).
My friend lives in Moscow. — usual / permanent situation.
My friend is staying with us this week. — temporary situation.

Negative & questions: don’t/doesn’t + V1; Do/Does + subject + V1? · am/is/are not + V-ing; Am/Is/Are + subject + V-ing?

Spelling: make → making; dance → dancing; run → running; lie → lying. Stative verbs such as like, love, know, understand and want are not usually used in Continuous: “I like dancing.”

🎤 Guided Speaking: My Friends & Free Time

Prepare a 1–2 minute answer. Use the questions as a guide.

  1. What do you usually do in your free time?
  2. Do you prefer spending time alone or with friends? Why?
  3. What hobbies are you interested in?
  4. How often do you meet up with your friends?
  5. What do you and your friends have in common?
  6. What are you doing these days in your free time?
  7. Are you learning or trying anything new at the moment?
  8. Are you and your friends planning anything special for the next few weeks?

Useful language

In my free time, I usually…Обычно в свободное время я…
I’m really interested in…Я очень интересуюсь…
We get along well because…Мы хорошо ладим, потому что…
At the moment, I’m…В данный момент я…
These days, we’re…В последнее время мы…
We have a lot in common.У нас много общего.
